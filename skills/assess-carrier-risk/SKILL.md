# Assess carrier risk

Read-only. Report indicators with their evidence; a person decides. Never contact the carrier, change a load or carrier, or call anyone a fraud. Never show bank details: say only that payment details changed, and when.

## 1. Identify

The carrier by name, MC or DOT number, or the carrier assigned to the load the user names.

## 2. Check

Note the evidence for every finding, and what you could not check.

- **Standing:** status (do not use ends the check: high risk), whether the carrier was added manually instead of being vetted, the vetting assessment result, and broker authority held by a carrier (a double-brokering sign).
- **History with us:** total and active loads. A first-time carrier on a valuable load is a risk; so are past claims and past security alerts on its loads.
- **Cargo cover:** the cargo insurance limit against the load's declared value; no cargo policy; a policy expiring before delivery; a temperature-controlled load without refrigeration breakdown cover. A missing declared value hides this check, so say so.
- **Capacity:** the carrier's trucks against its active loads whose pickup-to-delivery windows overlap, and the same truck, trailer or driver on overlapping loads. A small carrier holding more loads than it can drive is a strong sign.
- **Equipment:** a truck or VIN on the load that is not in the carrier's fleet.
- **Changes:** from the load's history, a carrier, truck, trailer, driver or driver phone changed after dispatch, especially close to pickup; carrier payment or factoring details changed shortly before payment.
- **Contacts:** a carrier contact on a free webmail address or on a domain other than the carrier's.
- **Driver:** CDL not verified.
- **Price and timing:** carrier pay far below lane pricing or historical rates; carrier assigned shortly before pickup; pickup on a Friday evening, weekend or holiday; a commodity thieves target (electronics, alcohol, food and beverage, metals, pharmaceuticals, tyres).
- **Tracking:** open alerts about simulated GPS, lost signal, off route, stopped, location mismatch, a do-not-use driver, double brokering or CDL verification; more than one tracker on the load in iTracker; tracking that started only after pickup.

## 3. Report

Lead with a level and the reason:

- **High:** do not use, cargo cover below the declared value, simulated GPS, a truck outside the carrier's fleet, or several strong signs together.
- **Elevated:** one strong sign or several weak ones.
- **Low:** nothing notable.

Then each finding with its evidence, what could not be checked (missing data or permissions), and next steps, for example: call back on the carrier's number from FMCSA, not one from an email; require live tracking before release; refuse last-minute truck or driver swaps; hold payment until a payment change is verified; escalate to a manager.
